The question

When does hand-made feature engineering stop being enough?

Modi was used to write Marathi for about seven centuries, and most of the archive is unread. This project recognises single handwritten characters with three handcrafted descriptors and classical models, explains every prediction with SHAP, and measures how much training data a plain CNN needs to overtake it.
