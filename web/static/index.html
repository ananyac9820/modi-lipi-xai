The new idea

A specialist for every look-alike pair

Modi was used to write Marathi for centuries, and most of the archive is unread. Paper after paper on recognising it says look-alike characters are the hard part. So when our SVM is stuck between two look-alikes, a small specialist makes the final call, looking only at the regions of the character that SHAP says separate those two.
